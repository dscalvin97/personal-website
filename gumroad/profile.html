About

I’m Calvin — fullstack developer by day, 3D nerd on the side, and a crocheter who takes tension and topology personally.

Patterns here are written for people who want to finish a project without decoding a wall of abbreviations. If a step matters, it’s in the PDF. If it doesn’t, it isn’t.
